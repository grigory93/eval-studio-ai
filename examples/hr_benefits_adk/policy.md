# Enterprise HR Benefits & Leave Policy

1. **Paid Time Off (PTO)**: Full-time employees accrue 18 days of PTO annually (1.5 days/month). Up to 5 days can roll over to the next year.
2. **Health, Dental & Vision**: Enrolled plan options include Standard PPO, HDHP with HSA, and Premium HMO.
3. **401(k) Retirement**: Company matches 100% up to 4%, plus 50% on the next 2% (max 5% company match).
4. **Parental Leave**: Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave; secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks.
